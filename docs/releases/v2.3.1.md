# RAGOps v2.3.1

- `strict_grounding` now treats antonym pairs and mismatched proper nouns as contradictions and no longer splits numbers such as `1,000`. On a 97-pair, author-labeled benchmark it scores 93% versus 72% for `lexical_groundedness` (see [evaluator accuracy](../evaluator-accuracy.md) for caveats and known failures).
- Updates CodeQL to 4.39.3, the container base image to Python 3.15-slim and `setup-uv` to 10.3.3.
- The Windows no-clone CI job now verifies the published 2.2.1 package.

## Compatibility

Unchanged from 2.2.x: `lexical_groundedness`, contracts, exit codes and decisions do not change; the stricter evaluator is opt-in. 2.3.0 was skipped to avoid a PyPI filename that may have been used before.
